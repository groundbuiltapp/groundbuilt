A few things people ask

Fair questions. Plain answers.

Do I need an email platform already?

No. If you have one, Groundbuilt can start there. If not, Groundbuilt will help you find a straightforward option that fits your list and budget. The platform’s own fee is separate from Groundbuilt’s service.

How long does setup take?

Usually a couple of weeks, depending on how quickly Groundbuilt can gather your details and get access to the right tools. Groundbuilt will share a clear timeline before work begins.

Will the emails sound like my business?

That’s the point. Groundbuilt uses your words, your offers, and the way you actually talk to customers. You get to review and approve everything before it goes live.

Am I locked into monthly management?

No. The $199 setup is a one-time project. Ongoing management is optional at $99 per month, and you can stop with 30 days’ notice.

What kind of business is this for?

Independent shops, studios, and service businesses that want to stay in touch with their customers without adding another daily task. If you’re not sure whether it fits, just ask.
