Questions?

Which email platform do you work with?

I work with most major email platforms. We'll figure out which one makes sense for your business during our strategy call. If you don't have a platform yet, I'll recommend based on your needs and budget.

How long before I see results?

Sequences launch in 2-3 weeks. You'll see impact within 30-60 days depending on your list size and audience engagement.

What if I'm not happy?

First 30 days are a trial period. If the sequences aren't working for you, no hard feelings. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice after that.

Do you manage my entire email program?

It depends on what you need. I can handle:

  • Newsletters
  • Promotions
  • Seasonal campaigns
  • Custom projects

Everything is negotiable. Let's talk about your specific goals.

What about email platform costs?

Platform fees are separate from my service fee. Most run $50-300/month depending on your list size, so factor that into your budget.

How do we get started?

Send me a message with your business type and current email setup (if any). We'll schedule your strategy call to map out sequences, then I'll handle the build. After launch, you stay on the monthly plan for ongoing management.

What if I just want a one-time project?

You can do the $399 setup and sequences, then stop. But I'd recommend staying on monthly management so I can optimize based on real performance data and fix things as they come up.
